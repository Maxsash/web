Star positions, magnitudes and colour indices are adapted from the HYG Database v4.1
by Astronomy Nexus (https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database).

The source catalogue is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

This site's stars-hyg-v41.bin selects stars of apparent magnitude 5.0 or brighter,
omits entries without a colour index, and quantizes right ascension, declination,
magnitude and colour index. The adapted data file is distributed under the same
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license.
